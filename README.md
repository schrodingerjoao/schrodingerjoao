## 🐺 The Lord of the North — Who Am I

I am **João Paulo Fagundes**, a **Software Engineer** from the realm of Brazil, sworn to the craft of building digital kingdoms from nothing but logic and determination. Though my watch has recently begun — less than a year on the battlefield — I march forward armed with both **blade and spell**: the dark arts of **Backend** and the luminous craft of **Frontend**.

Like Jon Snow, I knew nothing once. Now I forge APIs, raise sites from bare earth, conjure dashboards from raw data, and read the whispers hidden within numbers.

> *"The night is dark and full of bugs — and I debug them all."*
